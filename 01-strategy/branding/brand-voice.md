- **Personality (3 words):** Honest, practical, on-your-side
- **We sound like:** A senior from your college who now works at Infopark and has seen how hiring works. They tell you the straight truth over chai, show you the fix, and don't charge you for it. Calm and specific, never preachy. We explain *why* (for example "ATS software reads text, not pictures") and then give the next step.
- **We never sound like:** A placement-agency ad ("100% JOB GUARANTEE!!"), a corporate HR circular, a hustle-bro guru, or a "free trial*" that hits a paywall at download. Never fear-mongering ("your resume is why you're unemployed"), never condescending about where someone studied or how long they've been searching.
- **Words we use:** free (really free), interview, ATS-friendly, real text, live preview, download, no watermark, no sign-up needed to start, 5 minutes, your resume, the recruiter, the bots, fix, tailor, version, review link, Gulf format, fresher, first job, career gap, "here's why"
- **Words we avoid:** guaranteed / 100% selection / "land your dream job" (we can't promise outcomes) · AI-powered / AI-written (no AI features have shipped; don't invent them) · revolutionary, game-changer, leverage, synergy, hack, unlock your potential · Pro, premium, upgrade, trial (the product is free) · "Dear candidate", "kindly", "do the needful" (corporate stiffness) · any claim, stat or testimonial that hasn't been sourced
- **Proof we can always use (true product facts):** free with no watermark and no download limits · live preview, so what you see is what downloads · single-column, real selectable text with standard headings (Summary, Experience, Education, Skills) · import your old PDF; it's parsed in your browser and never uploaded · Gulf/GCC, UK, US, EU and Dutch formats · matching cover letter builder · share link at resume.kedilo.com/your-name/… · peer review: friends or mentors pin comments right on your resume
- **Language:** English first, in simple Indian English. Short sentences. On Instagram and in Reels, light Manglish is fine when it sounds natural ("Fresher aano? This one's for you."). Never make fun of Malayalam or of accents, and never write a whole caption in Manglish. Use Indian context: ₹, lakhs, CGPA/percentage, KTU, Infopark, campus placement, notice period, "Gulf job".
- **Emoji / hashtag policy:** Emojis are a tool, not decoration: at most 2 per post on Instagram/Facebook and 0–1 on LinkedIn/X. Use functional ones (✅ ❌ 👉 📄), not 🔥🚀💯 walls. Hashtags: LinkedIn 3–5, Instagram 3–8 niche + local (#KochiJobs #KeralaJobs #InfoparkKochi #FresherJobs #ResumeTips #ATSResume #GulfJobs), Facebook 0–2, X 0–1. Never use #Hiring unless we're actually hiring.

## Voice rules (check every post)

1. **Lead with the reader's problem, not our product.** "Applied to 40 jobs, zero calls?" comes before "Kedilo Resume does X".
2. **Be specific.** "Use the heading 'Experience', not 'My Journey'" beats "Optimise your resume".
3. **Show, don't hype.** Before/after, a screenshot or a one-line fix beats adjectives.
4. **One post, one idea, one CTA.** The default CTA is "Build yours free at resume.kedilo.com (link in bio)".
5. **Respect the struggle.** Job hunting in Kerala is hard (high graduate unemployment, Gulf uncertainty). Be warm, never pitying.
6. **Free means free.** Say "free, no watermark, no download limit" plainly and never add an asterisk.

## Tone by channel

| Channel     | Tone |
| ----------- | ---- |
| LinkedIn    | The knowledgeable senior. Clear, practical and a little more formal. Short paragraphs with a strong first line before "see more". Teach one ATS or recruiter insight per post. Speak to freshers *and* the people who hire them (HRs, mentors at Infopark/Kakkanad firms). No Manglish. |
| Instagram   | The friendly chettan/chechi who's good at this. Punchy and visual-first: carousels ("5 resume mistakes Kochi recruiters see daily"), before→after and quick Reels. Light Manglish hooks are OK. Write captions people will save and send to a friend. |
| X / Twitter | Sharp and witty, short. Hot takes on hiring and ATS myths, job-hunt relatability and one-liners. Max 1 emoji. Reply-friendly and never snarky toward job seekers. |
| Facebook    | Warm and plain-spoken, for parents, Gulf-returnees and career-restarters, and in Kerala job and Gulf-job groups. Step-by-step help ("How to write a Gulf CV with a photo"). Simple English, with Malayalam in hooks when posting to Malayalam groups. No jargon. |

## Visual identity

- **Colors (hex):** primary `#4f46e5` (brand indigo, buttons/CTAs/highlight word) · secondary `#312e81` (deep indigo, headings on light, or as a dark background) · accent `#eef2ff` with `#c7d2fe` border (soft indigo pill/badge, like the site's "100% free · Unlimited downloads · ATS-ready" chip) · background `#ffffff` (alt section `#fafafa`) · text `#18181b`, muted text `#52525b`
- **Fonts:** heading **Plus Jakarta Sans** ExtraBold 800, tight tracking · body **Geist** Regular/Medium (fallback Arial). For resume mockups, use the real template fonts (Georgia, Lato, Poppins).
- **Logo:** text wordmark "Kedilo Resume" in Plus Jakarta Sans 800, followed by an indigo `.` dot (`#4f46e5`).
- **Image style:** Clean and airy, with lots of white space, rounded-2xl cards, thin zinc borders and pill badges, matching the website. Hero visuals are **real resume pages and real product UI** (live preview, review comments pinned on a resume, the Gulf template), not stock photos. When people appear, use authentic Kerala/Kochi settings (Kochi Metro, Infopark/Kakkanad offices, a college canteen, a home desk with a laptop), with natural light and real faces, not suited handshake stock. Use one bold headline per image, with one key word in indigo, readable on a phone. Before/after layout: ❌ on a muted grey left, ✅ in indigo on the right.

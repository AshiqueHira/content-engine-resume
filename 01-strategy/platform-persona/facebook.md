# Facebook: platform persona

## If Facebook were a person
A friendly, community-minded 30–55-year-old who checks the feed to keep up with friends, family and groups. They want **useful, trustworthy, relatable content** they can share with people they know. They're more likely to read a longer post and leave a comment than younger audiences are.

## Audience
- Broad and older-leaning (30+), parents, mid-career and career-changing professionals, local communities
- Very active in **Groups** (job boards, career-advice groups, local communities)
- Mindset: community, practical help, stability, sharing with people they know

## What the platform rewards
- **Meaningful interactions**: comments, conversations between people, shares to Messenger
- Reels and native video (strongly pushed, including to people who don't follow you)
- Content that starts discussion inside Groups
- Native content over links to other sites

## What it punishes
- Engagement bait ("Like if…, share if…"), which is explicitly reduced
- Clickbait headlines and posts that are mostly a link
- Low-quality reposted content

## Content that works
- Practical, step-by-step advice ("How to explain a career gap")
- Relatable stories and success stories
- Short Reels with tips
- Questions that start discussion in Groups
- Simple, clear graphics with one message

## Voice on Facebook
Warm, plain-spoken, encouraging and trustworthy. Explain things simply. Avoid jargon and too much slang.

## Format & specs
| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Text limit | Very high (~63k), but short posts (one line to 3 short paragraphs) do best |
| Visible before "See more" | ~125–150 characters on mobile |
| Feed image | 1080×1350 (4:5) or 1080×1080 (1:1) |
| Link preview image | 1200×630 |
| Reels / Stories | 1080×1920 (9:16) |
| Hashtags | 0–2 |

## Post checklist
- [ ] Clear, practical value in the first line
- [ ] Simple visual that's readable on a phone
- [ ] Invites a genuine reply (not engagement bait)
- [ ] Consider cross-posting to relevant Groups

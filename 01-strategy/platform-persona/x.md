# X (Twitter): platform persona

## If X were a person
A sharp, opinionated, always-online insider who reads the news first and has a take on it within minutes. They want **hot takes, quick insights, and to be part of the conversation**. They have no patience for fluff and enjoy wit.

## Audience
- Tech workers, founders, journalists, builders, marketers, and very online professionals
- Real-time: activity spikes around news, launches and trending topics
- Mindset: being early, being right, being clever

## What the platform rewards
- **Replies and conversation**, followed by reposts and bookmarks
- Timeliness: posts that ride a trending topic or news within hours
- Threads that teach something step by step
- Consistent posting and replying to bigger accounts in your niche
- Native media (images, short video) over plain links

## What it punishes
- External links, which get lower reach (put the link in a reply)
- Corporate, ad-sounding copy
- Overuse of hashtags (1 at most, or none)
- Posting nothing but promotion

## Content that works
- One-line hot takes and observations
- Threads: "I reviewed 500 resumes. Here's what the top 1% did 🧵"
- Quick tips with a screenshot
- Taking part in trending conversations with a useful angle
- Polls

## Voice on X
Concise, sharp and conversational. Personality matters more than polish. Lowercase-casual is fine. Every word must earn its place.

## Format & specs
| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Text limit | 280 characters (free accounts); Premium allows longer posts |
| Image | 1600×900 (16:9) or 1080×1350 (4:5); up to 4 images |
| Thread | Hook post + 4–10 replies; the last one has the CTA and link |
| Hashtags | 0–1 |
| Links | In a reply, not the main post |

## Post checklist
- [ ] Understandable without any context
- [ ] Takes a clear stance or gives a clear benefit
- [ ] Link (if any) goes in a reply
- [ ] Fits the timing (news, or a time when the audience is online)

# Instagram: platform persona

## If Instagram were a person
A visual, fast-scrolling, aspirational 20-something with a thumb that never stops. They want to **be inspired, entertained, or saved some time**, and they decide in under a second based on how a post looks. They **save** posts they want to come back to and **share** posts that say something about them.

## Audience
- Mostly 18–34; students, early-career people, creators, small business owners
- Scrolls in the evening, at night, and in short breaks throughout the day
- Mindset: self-improvement, aesthetics, relatability, "this is so me"

## What the platform rewards
- **Shares (DM sends) and saves**, which are the strongest signals, followed by watch time on Reels
- Reels for reaching people who don't follow you yet; carousels for saves and engagement from followers
- Original content. Reposts and content carrying another app's watermark get reduced reach
- Consistent visual style and frequent posting

## What it punishes
- Text-heavy, cluttered images that can't be read on a phone
- Watermarks from other apps (e.g. TikTok)
- Engagement bait and irrelevant hashtags
- Links in captions, which can't be clicked (use the bio link or story link stickers)

## Content that works
- Swipeable carousels: "5 resume mistakes", "Before → After", "Save this for later"
- Relatable memes about job hunting or work life
- Short Reels: quick tips, screen recordings, transformations
- Quote or text-on-image posts with strong design
- Stories: polls, behind-the-scenes, questions from the audience

## Voice on Instagram
Casual, warm and punchy. Emojis are fine in moderation. Speak like a friend who's good at this. Lead with the visual; the caption supports it.

## Format & specs
| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Caption limit | 2,200 characters |
| Visible before "more" | ~125 characters |
| Feed image / carousel | 1080×1350 (4:5) recommended; the profile grid crops previews to 3:4 |
| Carousel | Up to 20 slides; 6–10 is ideal; slide 1 = hook, last slide = CTA |
| Reels / Stories | 1080×1920 (9:16); keep text within the safe zone (away from the top and bottom) |
| Hashtags | 3–5 relevant ones (Instagram now caps the number) |
| Links | Bio link or a Story link sticker only |

## Post checklist
- [ ] First image or frame stops the scroll on its own
- [ ] Text readable on a phone (large font, high contrast)
- [ ] Gives a reason to save or share
- [ ] CTA: "Save this", "Send to a friend who…", or "Link in bio"

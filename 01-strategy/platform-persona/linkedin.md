# LinkedIn: platform persona

## If LinkedIn were a person
A driven, slightly formal professional with coffee in hand, scrolling between meetings. They want to **look smart, get ahead in their career, and learn something useful in 30 seconds**. They distrust hard selling and reward people who share real experience.

## Audience
- Working professionals, job seekers, recruiters, founders, managers, students entering the workforce
- Scrolls mostly on weekday mornings, at lunch, and early evening; quiet at weekends
- Mindset: career growth, credibility, and their own professional reputation

## What the platform rewards
- **Dwell time** (people stop and read) and **meaningful comments**, more than likes
- Personal stories with a professional lesson
- Expertise from real people. Posts from personal profiles beat posts from company pages
- Carousels (PDF documents), which people swipe through slowly
- Replying to comments quickly in the first hour after posting

## What it punishes
- External links in the post body, which reduce reach (put the link in the first comment if needed)
- Engagement bait ("comment YES if…"), too many hashtags, tagging people who aren't involved
- Generic corporate announcements and obviously AI-written filler

## Content that works
- "I made this mistake so you don't have to" stories
- Step-by-step frameworks and checklists
- Before/after comparisons (e.g. a resume rewrite)
- Contrarian but defensible opinions about work or hiring
- Data and insights from your own experience

## Voice on LinkedIn
Clear, confident and helpful. First person. Short lines with plenty of white space. Professional but human, with no corporate jargon.

## Format & specs
| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Text limit | 3,000 characters |
| Visible before "…see more" | ~210 characters, so the hook must land here |
| Image | 1200×1200 (1:1) or 1080×1350 (4:5) |
| Carousel | PDF upload, 1080×1350, 5–10 slides is the sweet spot |
| Hashtags | 0–3, specific |
| Best post types | Text + image, carousel, text-only story, short native video |

## Post checklist
- [ ] Hook in the first 1–2 lines
- [ ] One clear lesson or takeaway
- [ ] Ends with a question that invites real comments
- [ ] No link in the body

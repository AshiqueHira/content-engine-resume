# News & Trends: prompt

**Purpose:** Find what's happening right now that our content can ride on, as input for the strategy.
**Output:** Append findings to `data.md` in this folder.

## Prompt
You are a market and trends researcher for our product. Search for news and trends from the **last 7 days** that are relevant to our audience.

Look for:
1. **Industry news:** launches, policy changes, reports, layoffs/hiring shifts
2. **Social trends:** viral formats, memes, hashtags, recurring conversations on LinkedIn / Instagram / X
3. **Competitor moves:** new features, campaigns, messaging changes
4. **Seasonal / calendar hooks:** events, holidays and awareness days in the next 2–4 weeks

For each item:
- Give the source link and date. Skip anything you can't source.
- Explain in one line why it matters to our audience.
- Suggest one content angle we could take.
- Rate relevance from 1 to 5. Keep only items rated 3 or higher.

Finish with the **top 3 opportunities**, ranked.
